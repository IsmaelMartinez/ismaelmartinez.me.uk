    Controls

    Shooting is eight-way: you fire in the direction you push, standing still or on the move, and the gun keeps firing for as long as you hold it. One hand moves, the other aims. A gun you walk over swaps in on its own, and the machine gun comes back when its ammo runs out.

    Move

    Fire
    Hold the left button: the gun fires toward the pointer.

    Gamepad

    Move
    Left stick or D-pad
    Fire
    Right stick: push it the way you want to shoot.
    Pause
    P or Esc on the keyboard, or the ⏸ button above the screen.

    Touch

    Move
    Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen.
    Fire
    Drag anywhere on the right half: the way you drag is the way you shoot.
    Pause
    The ⏸ button above the screen.
