⚽ Calcio '90

Two groups. One cup. Sixty seconds a match.

Arrows or WASD move · Z shoot or slide · X cross · C pass or switch · P pause · Enter starts · a red marker means the goal is in range, a blue one means a header or volley is on

Pick one of twelve sides, come top two in your group, then win a semi-final and a final. A shoots or slides, B crosses, C passes or switches player. The marker at your feet and the arrow in the panel turn red when you are in shooting range; outside it, A hoofs the ball clear instead. They turn blue when a ball in the air is within your reach, and that is when A is a header or a volley rather than a slide tackle. A goal pays 100, a group win 400, the cup 2500. There is no save and no continue: a run is one sitting.
