STREET CHAMPION's fighter sprites, built by scripts/fighter-sprites.py.

Every fighter is drawn from puffolotti's "Mustermenschen" fighting-game characters
(Sprite created by Puffolotti Accident), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
  https://opengameart.org/content/musternenschen-v1-complete-collection

Bodies used, each recoloured into a fighter's costume and cut down to the frames the game plays:
  Antony Nagasaki (KENJI, ACE), Larissa Disaster (MEI), George Nguyen (DUKE), Byron Verlaine (VARGA),
  Danny Van Damage (BORIS, RAJ, ROCKO), Kelvin Zone (TORA, SAKDA), Kelvin Area (RIO, ZAR).

No sprite from the arcade game this cabinet homages is used.
